# Changelog

What changes in each version of Vinus Arcade. Versions follow [semantic versioning](https://semver.org).

## 0.3.1 (2026-11-14)

- **Your best run before joining the ranking counts**: it waits on your computer until you pick a
  nickname, the ranking shows where it would land, and it goes in with you. A run the server could
  not be reached for waits too, and goes in the next time you play or open the ranking.
- **A new best invites you to the ranking**: if you have not joined, the card of the record shows
  "See where it lands", which opens the board with your run in its place and the nickname box.

## 0.3.0 (2026-11-13)

- **World ranking**: the ten best runs of each game this week and of all time. Joining takes a
  nickname, and nothing is sent before that. The server plays each run again before it counts,
  so a score cannot be made up, and leaving deletes everything it kept.
- **Coins and the x2 tile in Blip**: coins sit in about half the gaps, at random, instead of every
  third one. The x2 tile is rare: it gets likelier the further a run goes (about one run in five
  of 20 bookshelves sees one), it hangs off the easy line, and the next one takes 40 bookshelves.
- **Characters and accessories cost more**, from 75 to 900 coins. What you bought before keeps
  the price you paid: your coins do not go down.
- **Privacy**, in the README: what the plugin does with the access Orca warns about, and what
  leaves your computer (only your nickname and your runs, if you join the ranking).

## 0.2.0 (2026-11-12)

- **More games**: a window on the card shows the games there are and the room kept for the ones
  on the way.
- **Language**: the panel starts in English and changes to Spanish in the options, instead of
  following the language of Orca.

## 0.1.0 (2026-11-12)

The first version.

- **Blip**, the first game: one button to fly an agent between the bookshelves of an office at
  dusk.
- **Your office**: your Orca projects hang on the wall with their own icons, and your running
  agents sit at the desks, typing, waiting for you, stuck or asleep after finishing.
- **The x2 tile**: found in a run now and then, it makes the coins you catch count double for 12
  seconds.
- **Your agents in the run**: an agent that finishes mid-run sends its project into the run to be
  caught.
- **Notices in the game's own voice** when an agent finishes or needs you, with one click to go
  to it.
- **Characters and accessories** bought with the coins of your runs, shared by every game.
- **Options**: sound, hiding project names, and whether the game pauses when an agent needs you
  or finishes.
- English and Spanish.
